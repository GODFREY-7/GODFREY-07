# Electrical & Electronic Engineering Student

I design and build practical engineering systems across **embedded electronics, sensing, automation, electromechanical design, CAD, and simulation**.

I am especially interested in taking engineering problems through the full process:

**Problem → Concept → Design → Prototype → Test → Improve**

## Featured Work

### Lab Automation System — In Development

Developing an electromechanical laboratory automation concept for handling and processing laboratory vials. Work includes mechanism design, CAD, motor selection, sensing, control, and prototype development.

### Electromagnetic Coupling Simulation

Built and analyzed a coupled-coil system in **ANSYS Maxwell 3D**, including geometry, windings, excitation setup, meshing, simulation, and magnetic-field evaluation at different separation distances.

### Motor Control & Characterization

Hands-on experimentation with DC and stepper motors, including coil identification, electrical measurements, transistor-based control concepts, protection circuitry, and prototype testing.

### 915 MHz Telemetry System

Developing and troubleshooting a wireless telemetry link using microcontrollers, UART communication, and 915 MHz radio modules for future remote-control and sensing applications.

### Undergraduate Research

Contributed to faculty-led civil/structural engineering research involving steel and concrete systems. Portfolio materials describe only my individual contributions and approved non-confidential work.
